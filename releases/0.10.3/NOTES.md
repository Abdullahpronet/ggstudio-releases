# GG Studio 0.10.3

- **Older Premiere Pro.** GG Studio now runs in Premiere Pro 2022 or newer (was 2026 or newer). Every tool works there: Animate, Interact, Scene, Color and Deep Glow.
- **Text and gaming graphics need Premiere Pro 2025 or newer.** They're Motion Graphics templates, and older Premiere can't open them. On 2022 to 2024 those buttons explain this instead of failing.
- **Templates rebuilt for Premiere 2025.** All 204 text and gaming templates were rebuilt so they open in Premiere Pro 2025 as well as 2026.
- **Deep Glow on Premiere before 25.6.** Wonder Glow only exists in Premiere 25.6 and newer. On older Premiere, Deep Glow uses a classic glow (stacked blur layers in Add mode) instead, and the presets show a short note.
- Effects that Adobe renamed ("Gaussian Blur (Legacy)", "Noise (Legacy)", "Linear Wipe (Legacy)", "Mosaic (Legacy)") are found under either name.
- After Effects is unchanged: 2022 or newer.
