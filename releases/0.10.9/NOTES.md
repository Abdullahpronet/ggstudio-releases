# GG Studio 0.10.9

- **New: GG Studio Preview panel.** The preview of the open tool in its own panel: float it, dock it anywhere, make it as big as you like.
  - Zoom with the mouse wheel or − / + (up to 800%), drag to move around, double-click or Fit to see it all.
  - Colour tools and DLSS 5 GG Booster: Before / Split / After and a split slider.
  - It follows the tool, the settings and the clip you select, at a higher resolution than the small preview, so zooming in stays sharp.
  - Open it with the new button next to each tool's name, or Window › Extensions › GG Studio Preview.
- After updating, restart After Effects / Premiere Pro once so the new panel appears.
