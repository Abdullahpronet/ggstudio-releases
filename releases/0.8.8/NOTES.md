# GG Studio 0.8.8

- **MW4 preset fixed for gameplay.** On bright gameplay the MW4 look came out washed out (about 60 % brighter, milky blacks, dark areas turning blue-grey). It now keeps the clip's colours and real blacks, with more contrast and detail.
- **Smarter colour for dark and bright clips.** The DLSS neural stage darkens and warms dark, cinematic footage but leaves normal gameplay alone. GG Studio now measures each clip first: dark clips get a version of the game look that keeps their shadows bright and colours true, and everything else gets the plain look. The progress line says when a clip is treated as dark.
