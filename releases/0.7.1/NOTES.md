## GG Studio 0.7.1

- **Live preview in every section.** The preview at the top of each tool now plays your own settings, and it restarts as soon as you change a slider, colour, toggle or style. Hover a style to see it before you pick it.
  - **Animate:** UI stagger, Gradient wipe, Depth reveal, Split reveal, Pixel dissolve and Particle dissolve.
  - **Text:** Word swap captions, Text grid, Tape label, Karaoke, Kill feed, Damage numbers, Combo counter, Banners, Number roll, Comment call to action and Text exploder.
  - **Interact:** 3D cursor, Click effects, Hover grid and Sound effects.
  - **Scene:** Card carousel, 3D ring, Animated background, Reformat, Glass and Prompt scene.
  - **Color:** Color FX and One colour now update the "after" picture live. The Strength slider in Shot match and Import LUT blends live too.
- Text animations and Text presets keep their live previews from 0.7.0.

Update from the banner in GG Studio, or run [GG-Studio-Setup-0.7.1.exe](GG-Studio-Setup-0.7.1.exe).
