# GG Studio 0.9.0

- **Free 2-day demo.** New users can try every tool for 2 days without a key: install GG Studio, open the panel and press **Start 2-day free demo**. The 2 days start at that moment, and each computer gets one demo.
- **Time left in the panel.** While the demo runs, a bar at the top shows how long is left (for example "1 day 14 h left"), with **Pick a plan** and **Enter key**. It turns orange in the last 6 hours.
- **The demo locks itself when it ends**, even with the panel open, and shows "Your free demo has ended" with the Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime plans. Paste the key from your Gumroad receipt to unlock everything again.
- Buyers with a key see no change.
