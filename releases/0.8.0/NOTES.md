## GG Studio 0.8.0

- **DLSS presets.** The DLSS 5 GG Booster tab has 7 one-click presets above the stage cards: GG Recommended, Max Detail, Fast, Upscale to 4K, Smooth 120 FPS, Vivid Pop and Cinematic. Click one, and you can still fine-tune every stage afterwards. Smooth 120 FPS doubles clips that are already 120 fps or more.
- **Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime plans.** GG Studio now works with all three kinds of key. Keys you already have keep working as Lifetime. Monthly and Yearly keys are checked online once a day. A cancelled plan keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.
- **Fixed:** the Gameplay fix tile in Color correction now shows its picture.

Update from the banner in GG Studio, or run [GG-Studio-Setup-0.8.0.exe](GG-Studio-Setup-0.8.0.exe).
