# GG Studio 0.10.0

- **GG Studio now works in Premiere Pro.** Open it from Window > Extensions > GG Studio in Premiere Pro 2022 or newer. Same panel, same licence key. Premiere gets the tools that fit an edit:
  - **Text:** Text presets (32 looks), Text animations, Text exploder, Word swap captions, Karaoke captions, Text grid, Tape label and Comment call to action. They arrive as Motion Graphics templates, so you change the words and colours in Essential Graphics.
  - **Gaming:** Kill feed, Damage numbers, Combo counter, Victory banner and Number roll.
  - **Color:** Color correction with Auto fix, Color grading, Color FX, One colour, Shot match and Import LUT.
  - **Animate and scene:** Clip entrances, Wipe, Pixel, Animated background, Reformat and Sound effects.
  - **DLSS 5 GG Booster** works on Premiere clips too.
- **New Deep Glow tab (Premiere).** A rich glow on the bright parts of your clips, built from four of Premiere's own Wonder Glow effects (Premiere Pro 25.6 or newer), so it needs no extra plugin. It also comes as four drag-on effect presets (Natural, Neon, Dreamy, Hot): click **Get the Deep Glow presets** in the tab, then import the file once in Premiere's Effects panel.
- **Deep glow on graphics.** Every Premiere text and gaming graphic has a Deep glow setting, with an optional glow colour.
- The installer now asks you to close Premiere Pro as well as After Effects.
