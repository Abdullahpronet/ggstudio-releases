# GG Studio 0.10.1

- **Premiere Pro 2026 or newer.** GG Studio's text and gaming graphics are Motion Graphics templates made in After Effects 2026, and Premiere can't open templates made in a newer version than itself. GG Studio now shows up only in Premiere Pro 2026 and newer, so nobody gets graphics that fail to load. After Effects still works from 2022.
- The installer says which versions it supports and mentions the free 2-day demo.
- Everything from 0.10.0: GG Studio in Premiere Pro, the Deep Glow tab and its four drag-on presets, and a Deep glow setting on every Premiere graphic.
