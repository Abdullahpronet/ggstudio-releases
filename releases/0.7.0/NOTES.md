## GG Studio 0.7.0

- **New: Text presets.** 32 text looks with a live preview, built on a new animation engine:
  - smart reveal, bounce and whole-line motion, with an even per-letter delay and smooth easing;
  - colour that fills the letters as they arrive, a glow around the colour, and a motion trail (echo) behind them;
  - In, Out, or In + Out on one layer;
  - four groups: Glow trails, Clean, Reel (in + out pairs) and More.
- Every setting can be tuned live: speed, delay, letters/words/lines, distance, direction, order, blur, rotation, start size, spacing, bounce, colour, glow and trail.
- **Ember rise rebuilt** on the new engine: letters now tilt in as they rise, arrive in red-orange and leave an orange trail.
- **Cleaner Text panel:**
  - tools are grouped into Animate text, Captions and Gaming;
  - every preview now sits on the same clean dark card instead of gameplay footage.

Update from the banner in GG Studio, or run [GG-Studio-Setup-0.7.0.exe](GG-Studio-Setup-0.7.0.exe).
