## GG Studio 0.6.0: Colour pack and Text pack

### Color
- **10 new looks:**
  - game looks: Valorant Pop, Warzone Cold, Fortnite Vivid, CoD Gritty, Apex Desert, Night Raid;
  - film looks: Film 2383, Portra Warm, Bleach Bypass, 90s VHS.
- **Color FX:** halation, bloom, colour fringe, grain, vignette and split-toning, each with its own strength.
- **Auto fix** in Color correction: it measures your clip and sets exposure, white balance and contrast for you.
- **Shot match:** clips from different recordings match the look of one reference clip.
- **One colour:** make a single colour pop, shift it to another, or keep only that colour.
- **Protect skin tones** in Color grading, for facecam clips.
- **Import LUT:** use your own .cube or .3dl file, with a strength slider.

### Text
- **10 new text styles:** Glitch, Slide up, 3D flip, Outline fill, Neon, Shine, Elastic, Squash, Slam, Zoom punch.
- **Karaoke captions:** the word being said lights up, TikTok style.
- **Gameplay text tools:**
  - kill feed;
  - damage numbers;
  - combo counter;
  - Victory / Rank up / MVP / Defeat banner;
  - number roll;
  - Comment "GG" call to action.

Update from the banner in GG Studio, or run [GG-Studio-Setup-0.6.0.exe](GG-Studio-Setup-0.6.0.exe).
