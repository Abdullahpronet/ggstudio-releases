# GG Studio 0.8.7

- **New game preset: MW4.** The bright MW4 "DLSS 5" look from the TikTok clips: full neural detail and a brighter, punchier picture that keeps your clip's own colours. Pick **MW4** in the Game row of the DLSS 5 GG Booster tab, then any Quality (Fast, HD, 4K, 4K · 120 FPS). It works on vertical clips too (2160×3840 at 4K).
- **Truer colour in every game look.** The DLSS neural stage adds a yellow-orange tint, mostly in the shadows. Every game look now removes it, so results keep the colours of the original clip.
- **Black borders stay black** in every game look (letterbox bars no longer turn brown).
- **Call of Duty** stays as the darker, calm "real life" cinematic look.
