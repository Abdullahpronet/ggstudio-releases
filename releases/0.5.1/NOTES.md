## GG Studio 0.5.1

- **Automatic updates.** GG Studio now tells you when a new version is out. Click **Update**, then restart After Effects, and you're on the new version.
- The DLSS 5 GG Booster tab now credits its engine: Visual Enhancer by Merserk, with a link to his repo.

**New buyers:** run [GG-Studio-Setup-0.5.1.exe](GG-Studio-Setup-0.5.1.exe).
**On 0.5.0:** run the installer once. Future versions update themselves.
