## GG Studio 0.7.2

- **Cleaner Animate, Interact, Scene and Color sections**, laid out like the Text section:
  - tools are grouped: Reveal and Transitions; Cursor and clicks and UI motion; 3D cards, Backgrounds and glass, Scenes and format; Fix and match and Looks.
  - every card and style tile now shows the tool on the same dark GG Studio card with a soft colour glow. Hover a card or tile to play it.
  - gameplay and photo previews (Depth reveal, Particle dissolve, Animated background, Glass, Prompt scene) sit as a rounded picture on that card.
  - Color cards show a before/after split; style tiles show the look itself, and the main before/after preview is framed the same way.
- Card and tile pictures are drawn from the live preview, so they always match your settings.

Update from the banner in GG Studio, or run [GG-Studio-Setup-0.7.2.exe](GG-Studio-Setup-0.7.2.exe).
