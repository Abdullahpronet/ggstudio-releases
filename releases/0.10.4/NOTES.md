# GG Studio 0.10.4

- **DLSS 5 GG Booster is about twice as fast.** The game looks' colour and sharpening are now done while the clip is prepared, so the engine only runs the DLSS 5 neural stage and the upscale. Tested on an RTX 5070 with 1080p 120 fps gameplay: 3 seconds of Call of Duty · HD went from 45 seconds to about 24, with the same colour and detail.
- **Live preview of your own clip.** The GG Booster tab's preview now shows the frame under the playhead, Original | DLSS 5 with a slider. It updates by itself (about 3 seconds) when you move the playhead or change the game, quality or a setting. Switch it off with Live in the corner of the preview.
- **The enhanced clip lands sooner.** It goes into your comp as soon as the engine is done; the light copy for smooth playback is made in the background and switches on by itself.
- The progress line shows the engine's percent.
- Encoding quality "Max" now says it's about twice as slow and makes files about 20 times bigger. "Best" looks the same.
- Setup: the GG Booster engine download is more reliable (retries when GitHub is busy, another drive when C: is full).
