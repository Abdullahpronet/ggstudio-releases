# GG Studio 0.8.1

- **Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime on one page.** All three plans are now picked on the GG Studio page on Gumroad. Monthly lasts 30 days and Yearly 12 months from the day you buy. They don't renew on their own.
- **Reminders before a plan ends.** Five days before a Monthly or Yearly plan runs out, a bar at the top of the panel says so, with a button to get a new plan. When it ends, paste the key from your new receipt and carry on.
- **Kinder handling of subscription payments** (keys from the earlier Monthly / Yearly subscription): a failed renewal payment keeps working for 7 days while you update your card, and renewing unlocks the panel with one click (Check again). Help has a link to manage your plan.
- **Smooth 120 FPS** now works on clips that are already 120 fps or more (it doubles them).
